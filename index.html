Zibrax Studio — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data. By using Zibrax Studio, you agree to this Policy.

What Data We Collect

We collect the following data:

  • Account Information: Email and name (via Clerk login)
  • User Content: AI Agents, prompts, knowledge base, files, and chat history
  • Usage Data: IP address, device information, browser type, and usage logs

This data is used only to provide the service, ensure security, and improve performance.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data only for:

  • Providing and managing the service
  • Security, fraud detection, and abuse prevention
  • Improving the platform and providing support

We never use your data for AI training, selling, or profiling.

Chat Data & AI Processing

Your chats with AI Agents are automatically processed to generate responses.

By default, no human reads your chats.

However, authorized staff may access chats in limited cases such as:

  • Security or abuse investigation
  • System debugging
  • Legal obligations or government requests

All such access is restricted and purpose-based.

Third-Party Services (Data Processors)

We use the following third-party services:

  • Clerk — Authentication and login
  • Cloudflare D1 — Database and data storage
  • Vercel — Hosting and analytics
  • AI Providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.) — AI response generation

These providers process data only for service delivery and follow their own data protection policies.

Data Storage & International Transfer

Your data is stored on Cloudflare D1 and other trusted infrastructure providers.

These servers may be located in different countries. We use industry-standard safeguards (such as contractual clauses) when transferring data.

You have the right to:

  • Access your data

We usually respond within 30 days.

Data Retention

We keep your data as long as your account is active.

After account deletion, we permanently remove your data within a reasonable time (maximum 30 days), unless we are required to keep some data for legal or security reasons.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures such as encryption and access controls to protect your data.

However, no internet-based system can guarantee 100% security.

  • Essential Cookies — Required for login and system operation (cannot be disabled)
